Homework: TouchDesigner practice

Practice TouchDesigner
Use TouchDesigner for at least 3 * 30 minute sessions. 
Familiarise yourself with the UI, look through the example project files, try changing parameters, adding nodes etc…. Don’t worry about breaking things, this is learning/experimentation!

Tutorial
Find and complete at least 1 * tutorial. 
Upload the completed TouchDesigner file, along with a link to the tutorial to your Github.
Submit the link to eclass.
