Vera Molnar's Carrés en deux positions 4 (2013) is an intriguing exploration of geometric abstraction and computational art. In this piece, Molnar combines rigid, structured forms with subtle shifts in positioning, creating a dynamic tension between order and transformation. The use of squares, a fundamental geometric shape, in varying alignments draws attention to the relationship between stability and change, allowing the viewer to explore movement within the confines of precision. I recreated this for part 1 and changed some visual effects for part 2 depending on the mouse movement.
